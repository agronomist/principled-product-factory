# Sommelier Table Pitch & JOLT Deal Desk (`/sommelier-table-pitch`)

Why do kitchens fall into the Build Trap? Because **"Order-Taking Waiter" ("Relationship Builder")** sales reps cave to every diner's off-menu custom request and discount demand just to be agreeable. Matthew Dixon & Brent Adamson's *The Challenger Sale* proves that **Relationship Builders account for only 4% of top performers in complex B2B sales, while Challengers (Master Sommeliers who guide the table) account for 54%**.

## 1. Pillar 1: Teach the Palate (The 6-Step Commercial Teaching Choreography)

**Rule**: Never open by reciting your menu! Reciting product features in Steps 1–5 turns you into a commodity diner competing on price.

Structure every commercial pitch in exact 6-step order:
1. **The Amuse-Bouche (Warmer)**: Validate the guest's world using benchmarked observations across peer kitchens. **Never ask the lazy cliché *"What keeps you up at night?"***.
2. **The Palate Cleanser (Reframe)**: Introduce a counter-intuitive insight showing that what they thought was normal kitchen waste is actually a structural blind spot leaking margin or speed.
3. **Rational Drowning (Quantified Cost of Spoilage / Inaction)**: Calculate their exact annual **Cost of Inaction ($\text{COI}_{\text{annual}}$)** using their own numbers. Require:
   $$\frac{\text{COI}_{\text{annual}}}{\text{Annual Contract Price}} \ge 3.0\times \quad (\text{Payback on Spoilage} \le 4.0\text{ months})$$
4. **Emotional Impact**: Humanize how that broken status quo burns out their line cooks and engineers every Sunday night.
5. **A New Way**: Define the vendor-agnostic kitchen architecture required to fix the reframe—*still without pitching brand or dish names*.
6. **Our Signature Pairing (Solution Map)**: Prove how our unique kitchen moats ([`../signature-dish-moat/SKILL.md`](../signature-dish-moat/SKILL.md)) are the only way to execute Step 5.

## 2. Pillar 2: Pair to Every Seat at the Table (Tailor for Resonance)

Translate the same commercial reframe into four distinct stakeholder pairings:
- **`ECONOMIC_BUYER_CFO`**: Monthly Cost-of-Delay spoilage, Food-Cost Margin $\ge 65\%$, ROIC, and $< 4$-month payback.
- **`TECHNICAL_ARCHITECT_VP_ENG`**: Zero single-supplier lock-in, 3-tier pantry fallback clean sheet, and 100% Liskov/Parnas verification.
- **`MOBILIZER_CHAMPION`** (*Go-Getters, Teachers, Skeptics*—never passive *Talkers*): A 14-day Test-Kitchen pop-up producing a cross-vendor audited `decisions.tsv` prep log they can hand directly to their executive committee.
- **`END_USER_OPERATOR`**: Axis 1 Prep Elimination ($\ge 0.85$) with passive plate-back learning and zero survey drudgery.

## 3. Pillar 3: Take Control of the Table (Constructive Tension = Anti-Build-Trap + Give-Get)

A great Sommelier holds **Constructive Tension** around the menu and the bill:
- **Off-Menu Feature Pushback**: If a prospect demands a custom one-off dish as a condition of signing, evaluate it with [`../no-empty-calories/SKILL.md`](../no-empty-calories/SKILL.md). If it fails Ulwick's Opportunity Score ($< 10.0$) or is a single-table whim ($N=1$), politely refuse the off-menu distraction, re-anchor the guest on the core 90-day ROI, and log the request in [`../dining-room-feedback/SKILL.md`](../dining-room-feedback/SKILL.md).
- **Give-Get Check Control**: Never comp a discount for free. Any price adjustment must:
  1. Pass [`../food-cost-guard/SKILL.md`](../food-cost-guard/SKILL.md) at the discounted price, AND
  2. Extract a concrete **Give-Get concession** (24-month commitment, annual upfront payment, and a joint public case study).

## 4. Cure Menu Paralysis (*The JOLT Effect*)

40–60% of B2B tables walk out without ordering (**"No Decision"**) due to **FOMU (Fear of Messing Up)**. When a table stalls, never dial up FOMO threats; execute the 4 **JOLT** moves:
- **J — Judge the Indecision**: Diagnose whether the stall is Menu Overload, Endless Tasting-Sample Creep, or Fear of Food Poisoning (Outcome Fear).
- **O — Offer the Sommelier's Recommendation**: Take away the 4-page wine list and prescribe the exact starting pairing.
- **L — Limit the Tasting**: Timebox evaluation to 14 days on our 10 golden recipes + 1 live harness tasting. Refuse 90-day free catering banquets.
- **T — Take Risk Off the Table**: Offer the 3-tier pantry fallback SLA, a 6-month re-pricing retro-audit clause, and a Stage A exit ramp.
