# Learn

1. Name the mistake and the evidence for it.
2. Keep it only if it will still matter in six months and changes what happens next.
3. Fix it at the highest level that works:
   design that makes it impossible, then types, then a lint or hook whose error names
   the fix, then a test, and prose last.
4. Show the new check failing on the original mistake and passing after the fix.
5. Before editing any skill or CLAUDE.md, show the proposed change and wait for approval.

Also delete: remove rules and features that have not earned their upkeep.
