# Challenger Deal Desk & JOLT Pipeline (`/challenger-deal-desk`)

Why do software companies fall into the Build Trap? Because **"Relationship Builder"** sales reps acquiesce to every prospect's bespoke feature request and discount demand just to be agreeable. Matthew Dixon & Brent Adamson's *The Challenger Sale* proves that **Relationship Builders account for only 4% of high performers in complex B2B sales, while Challengers account for 54%**.

## 1. Pillar 1: Teach for Differentiation (The 6-Step Commercial Teaching Choreography)

**Rule**: Never lead with your product features! Pitching product capabilities in Steps 1–5 commoditizes you into an RFP checkbox war.

Structure every commercial pitch in exact 6-step order:
1. **The Warmer**: Validate the customer's world using benchmarked observations across peer deployments. **Never ask the lazy cliché *"What keeps you up at night?"***.
2. **The Reframe**: Introduce a counter-intuitive insight showing that what they thought was a normal cost of doing business is actually a structural blind spot leaking margin or velocity.
3. **Rational Drowning (Quantified Cost of Inaction)**: Calculate their exact annual **Cost of Inaction ($\text{COI}_{\text{annual}}$)** using their own numbers. Require:
   $$\frac{\text{COI}_{\text{annual}}}{\text{Annual Solution Price}} \ge 3.0\times \quad (\text{Payback on Bleed} \le 4.0\text{ months})$$
4. **Emotional Impact**: Humanize how that status-quo failure mode burns out their engineering or operations team day-to-day.
5. **A New Way**: Define the vendor-agnostic architectural capabilities required to fix the reframe—*still without pitching SKU or brand names*.
6. **Our Solution Map**: Prove how our unique platform invariants are the only way to execute Step 5.

## 2. Pillar 2: Tailor for Resonance Across the Buying Committee

Translate the same commercial reframe into four distinct stakeholder lenses:
- **`ECONOMIC_BUYER_CFO`**: Monthly Cost-of-Delay bleed, Contribution Margin $\ge 65\%$, ROIC, and $< 4$-month payback.
- **`TECHNICAL_ARCHITECT_VP_ENG`**: Zero single-vendor lock-in, 3-tier SRE clean-sheet fallback, and 100% Liskov/Parnas verification.
- **`MOBILIZER_CHAMPION`** (*Go-Getters, Teachers, Skeptics*—never passive *Talkers*): A 14-day Stage A proof producing a cross-vendor audited `decisions.tsv` trail they can hand directly to their executive committee.
- **`END_USER_OPERATOR`**: Axis 1 Workflow Elimination ($\ge 0.85$) with passive diff learning and zero popup drudgery.

## 3. Pillar 3: Take Control of the Sale (Constructive Tension = Anti-Build-Trap + Give-Get)

Hold **Constructive Tension** around scope and price:
- **Bespoke Feature Pushback**: If a prospect demands a custom one-off feature as a condition of signing, evaluate it with [`../escape-the-build-trap/SKILL.md`](../escape-the-build-trap/SKILL.md). If it fails Ulwick's Opportunity Score ($< 10.0$) or is a single-account distraction ($N=1$), decline the custom build, re-anchor the buyer on the core 90-day ROI, and log the signal in [`../customer-flywheel/SKILL.md`](../customer-flywheel/SKILL.md).
- **Give-Get Price Control**: Never grant an untraded discount. Any price concession must:
  1. Pass [`../margin-guard/SKILL.md`](../margin-guard/SKILL.md) at the discounted unit price, AND
  2. Extract a concrete **Give-Get concession** (24-month commitment, annual upfront billing, and a joint public case study).

## 4. Overcome Customer Indecision (*The JOLT Effect*)

40–60% of B2B pipeline is lost to **"No Decision"** driven by **FOMU (Fear of Messing Up)**. When a buyer stalls, never dial up FOMO fear-mongering; execute the 4 **JOLT** moves:
- **J — Judge the Level of Indecision**: Diagnose whether the stall is Option Overload, Endless POC Creep, or Outcome Fear.
- **O — Offer a Bold Recommendation**: Eliminate 4-tier menu confusion and prescribe the exact starting configuration.
- **L — Limit the Exploration**: Timebox POC evaluation to 14 days on our golden dataset + 1 live harness journey. Refuse 90-day open-ended science projects.
- **T — Take Risk Off the Table**: Offer the 3-tier SRE fallback SLA, a 6-month re-pricing retro-audit clause, and a Stage A Bayesian exit ramp.
