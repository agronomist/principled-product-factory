# Principle: No Empty Calories

Making dishes 10x faster to cook makes **the Build Trap** (putting dishes on the menu nobody orders) 10x more dangerous.

- Reject any dish backed only by internal kitchen speculation (*"diners at some point will order it"*) or polite compliments (*The Mom Test*).
- Require the JTBD Opportunity Score $\text{Importance} + \max(\text{Importance} - \text{Satisfaction}, 0) \ge 10.0$ and verifiable guest skin-in-the-game (time commitment for Stage A Test-Kitchen pop-ups; reputation or financial/pain commitment for Stage B/C menu builds).
- Every dish must state a falsifiable guest behavior shift (`baseline -> target` within 30 days). If post-launch orders are $< 40\%$ of target and outcome lift is $< 25\%$ of promise, 86 the dish and delete the code.
